# World sound credits

Every file in `public/audio/world/` is cut from a recording released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication),
checked on the source page listed below. Files were trimmed, faded, high-passed, peak-normalized and encoded to mono MP3;
files with more than one source are a mix of those recordings.

Source collections:

- BigSoundBank (bigsoundbank.com): each sound page states "License CC0 (public domain)".
- OpenGameArt (opengameart.org): each listed pack page shows CC0 as its license.
- Kenney (kenney.nl): RPG Audio, Impact Sounds and Sci-Fi Sounds, all CC0.
- The Free Firearm Sound Library (Ben Jaszczak et al., freefirearmsfx.com), CC0: the first 60 ms of one shot is the crack of each explosion.
- OpenGameArt authors of packs added in the second round: EZduzziteh (Explosions), zq (Toy bricks).
