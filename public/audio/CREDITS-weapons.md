# Weapon sound credits

Every file in `public/audio/weapons/` is cut and processed (trimmed, filtered, layered, level-matched) from recordings released under CC0 1.0 or dedicated to the public domain. No attribution is required; this file records where each one came from.

Near gunshots are layered (crack, body, sub, mechanism) and carry only a short diffuse body made from the shot itself; their long tail is the map's reverb (`public/audio/space/`, see CREDITS-ambience.md). Far gunshots are the library's mid-distance microphone takes with their own reflections (nailer and harpoon: the near build softened, with a diffuse field made from the shot convolved with decaying noise). The falling tone in the pulse rifle and the Doppler curve of the bullet whiz are synthesized processing as well.
