# Ambience and map reverb credits

`public/audio/ambience/` holds each map's stereo bed (fortress and temple add a quieter second layer: forest birds, a stream) and the scenery one-shots scattered around the listener; `public/audio/space/` holds one stereo impulse response per map. All are cut and processed from recordings released under CC0 1.0. The impulse responses are the part of real outdoor gunshot recordings after the direct blast (range slap-back and decay), whitened by the blast's own spectrum and given a per-map decay, with a low end taken from a fireworks shell's rumble over a town; the game plays random stretches of each bed, cross-faded into each other.
